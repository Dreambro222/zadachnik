## Value-prop layer baked into every draft

Each email weaves the following five differentiators (which is what makes
these stronger than a typical cold pitch):

1. **Best in-market pricing** — direct OEM, no SA-channel markup. Indicative
   reference: 5–12% below typical landed cost. We show supplier invoices to
   back the claim.
2. **In-person + OEM-partner co-meetings** — we'll fly into SA for a working
   session, and bring a Chinese OEM partner (Chery / GWM / BYD / FAW depending
   on focus) along if relevant.
3. **Factory tours** — full 3-day Shanghai–Chongqing–Hangzhou itinerary at our
   cost, 5 OEM facilities, NDA-friendly.
4. **Logistics-ready** — containers + RoRo lots already running, FOB China /
   CIF Durban / Cape Town / PE, 6-week lead time.
5. **Maximum deal transparency** — open-book supplier invoices, inspection-
   on-arrival terms, escrow if helpful, references on request.

And a tonal element: **young + technically flexible** — code for "we adapt
deal structure to yours, not the other way round; pilot lots, non-standard
payment terms, fast iteration are all fine."

## Calibration loop — what to look for in replies

After 5–7 days of replies, look at:

1. **Which value-prop landed?** The pricing claim (5–12% below) vs the
   factory-tour offer vs the "fly in next week" vs the transparency angle —
   did replies cluster around one?
2. **Did the `reply tour` / `reply pricing` hook work?** One-word replies
   are the cleanest signal that the CTA structure is right; we keep that
   format in the Smartlead push.
3. **Which subject line opened best?** Subject A/B for the broader push:
       Option A: "Direct-from-China RHD supply for <COMPANY> — pricing
                  5–12% below SA channel"
       Option B: "<BRAND>/<BRAND> direct supply — factory tours + working
                  session in <CITY>"
4. **Which lead profile?** Dealer-group vs rental vs taxi-finance — where's
   reply-rate highest, and what's the modal objection?
5. **Did predicted personal emails bounce?** (e.g. `colin.lazarus@laz.co.za`)
   — confirms which patterns we shouldn't use in the Smartlead push.
6. **Objections — capture them for the playbook.** Common ones to expect:
   "we already have a Chinese supplier" → response: open-book invoice
   comparison; "lead time too long" → response: existing stock options
   (RoRo lots in transit); "deferred payment / escrow only" → response:
   yes, that's standard for us on first lot.

The Smartlead sequence template gets updated based on this signal —
specifically `first_touch.body` per priority bucket. We re-run `outreach
plan --refresh` to regenerate the playbooks, then push the next 60+ leads
with the refined copy.
