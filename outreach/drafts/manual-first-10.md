## Value-prop layer baked into every draft

Each email weaves the following five differentiators (the things that make
these stronger than a typical cold pitch):

1. **Best in-market pricing** — direct OEM, no SA-channel markup. Supplier
   invoices available on request as proof.
2. **In-person + OEM-partner co-meetings** — we'll fly into SA for a working
   session, and bring a Chinese OEM partner (Chery / GWM / BYD / FAW etc.)
   along if relevant.
3. **Factory tours** — full 3-day Shanghai–Chongqing–Hangzhou itinerary at our
   cost, 5 OEM facilities, NDA-friendly.
4. **Logistics-ready** — containers + RoRo lots already running, FOB China /
   CIF Durban / Cape Town / PE, 6-week lead time.
5. **Maximum deal transparency** — open-book supplier invoices, inspection
   on arrival, escrow if helpful, references on request.

Tonal element: **young + technically flexible** — code for "we adapt deal
structure to yours, not the other way round; test shipments, non-standard
payment terms, fast iteration are all fine."

## Per-lead CTA pattern

Every email asks two things — clear, low-friction:

1. **Which Chinese brands or models are you currently sourcing or considering?**
   → we send FOB pricing + lead times within 24 hours.
2. **Working session in [city] or a factory tour in China?**
   → we send 3 meeting times or 3 China-itinerary options.

(We deliberately don't quote prices in the cold email — pricing only makes
sense against specific models, and asking the prospect to name the models is
the most natural reason for them to reply.)

## Calibration loop — what to look for in replies

After 5–7 days of replies, look at:

1. **Which CTA landed?** The "list your models" ask vs the "working session" ask vs the "factory tour" ask — did replies cluster around one?
2. **Which models did they name?** This is the gold — every model named in a reply is a confirmed demand signal. The Smartlead sequence template should then mention those exact models in `first_touch.body`.
3. **Which subject line opened best?** For the broader push we'll subject-test:
       Option A: "Direct-from-China RHD supply for <COMPANY> — open-book pricing"
       Option B: "<BRAND_THEY_NAMED> direct supply — working session in <CITY>"
4. **Which lead profile?** Dealer-group vs rental vs taxi-finance — where's reply-rate highest, and what's the modal objection?
5. **Did predicted personal emails bounce?** (e.g. `colin.lazarus@laz.co.za`) — confirms which patterns we shouldn't use in the Smartlead push.
6. **Objections — capture them for the playbook.** Common ones to expect: "already have a Chinese supplier" → response: open-book invoice comparison; "lead time too long" → response: existing stock options (RoRo lots in transit); "want to see paperwork first" → response: NRCS LOA + OEM authorisation packs on request.

The Smartlead sequence template gets updated based on this signal —
specifically `first_touch.body` per priority bucket. We re-run `outreach
plan --refresh` to regenerate the playbooks, then push the next 60+ leads
with the refined copy.
