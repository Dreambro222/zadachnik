3. Why we process it (lawful purpose)

Processing is performed in pursuit of the Operator's legitimate business interests under POPIA s.11(1)(f) — namely, to make initial contact with companies that we reasonably believe may be interested in the wholesale supply of right-hand-drive Chinese-manufactured vehicles into Southern Africa.

The processing is strictly limited to that purpose. No data is sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

4. Direct marketing — your opt-out right (POPIA s.69)

You may opt out of further direct marketing at any time, free of charge, by any of the following means:

  • Replying to any of our emails with the single word UNSUBSCRIBE in the subject line or body;
  • Emailing ${UNSUBSCRIBE_EMAIL} with your company name;
  • Writing to the postal address above.

5. Retention

We retain outreach records (the email body, our personalisation notes, and any reply) for as long as the commercial relationship is active and for up to three years thereafter for compliance and audit purposes. Opt-out requests are retained indefinitely so we do not re-contact you.

7. Your rights under POPIA

You have the right to:

  • Request confirmation that we hold information about you (s.23);
  • Request access to that information (s.23);
  • Request correction or deletion of inaccurate information (s.24);
  • Object to processing (s.11(3)) — including all direct marketing;
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

To exercise any of these rights, email ${PRIVACY_EMAIL}. We respond within 30 days.
